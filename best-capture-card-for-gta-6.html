What to look for in a GTA 6 capture card

Rockstar Games hasn't published GTA 6's official PC specifications or any streaming and capture guidance, so treat any hardware pairing here as general best practice rather than a guaranteed requirement. That said, open-world titles at this scale tend to push high-bitrate, high-resolution capture, so a handful of specs matter more than others when you're shopping for a card.

  • Passthrough resolution and refresh — you want to preview gameplay at the same resolution and frame rate you're playing at, without a laggy monitor feed getting in the way of your driving or aiming.
  • HDR passthrough — if you're running an HDR display, a card that can pass HDR10 through cleanly avoids a washed-out or flickering preview image while you record.
  • Interface — internal PCIe cards typically offer the lowest latency; external USB cards trade a sliver of latency for portability, easier setup, and no case-opening required.
  • Hardware encoding — onboard H.264/HEVC encoding takes the load off your CPU and GPU so capture doesn't eat into your in-game frame rate.

Recommended capture settings for GTA 6

Rockstar hasn't released official capture, streaming, or performance guidance for GTA 6, so treat the following as general open-world game recording practice rather than a confirmed recommendation.

  • Match your recording resolution and frame rate to your in-game display settings to avoid scaling artifacts
  • Use a variable or high constant bitrate for busy city scenes with lots of on-screen detail and traffic
  • Enable HDR passthrough only if your capture card, monitor, and recording software all support it cleanly
  • Record to a fast, dedicated SSD to avoid dropped frames during long open-world sessions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a capture card to record GTA 6 on PC?

Not necessarily — many PC players use software like OBS to record directly from their GPU. A capture card matters most for console players, or for anyone recording from a second PC so their gaming rig isn't taxed by encoding while they play.

Will GTA 6 support 4K60 capture out of the box?

Rockstar hasn't confirmed GTA 6's supported resolutions or frame rates on any platform. Treat 4K60 as an aspirational capture target based on current-generation hardware trends, not a confirmed spec for the game.

Is an internal or external capture card better for streaming?

External cards are easier to set up and move between systems, while internal cards generally offer marginally lower latency. Either type works well for streaming GTA 6 once it releases — the right choice comes down to how much setup effort you want.
