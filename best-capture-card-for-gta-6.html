Why capture card specs matter for GTA 6

A capture card's job is to grab your gameplay signal — either before it reaches your display or from a second machine — without adding noticeable lag or dropping quality. For a graphically dense open-world game like GTA 6, that means wanting full 4K60 HDR10 passthrough if you're capturing from a console or a second PC, low added latency so a passthrough monitor doesn't feel delayed during fast driving or combat, and an encoder that can keep pace with quick camera pans through Vice City's streets without introducing visible compression artifacts.

Rockstar has not published official PC system requirements or confirmed HDR implementation details for GTA 6, and any claims you see online about the game's exact resolution targets, ray-tracing modes, or frame-rate options on PC should be treated as rumored, not confirmed, until Rockstar says otherwise. The specs compared on this page describe the capture hardware itself, not the game's performance.

How we chose these picks

We compared publicly listed specifications for internal and external capture cards from established brands with a track record in game capture, focusing on passthrough resolution, added latency, HDR10 support, interface type, and encoding options rather than marketing copy. The premium and all-round picks both support full 4K60 HDR10 passthrough because that's the ceiling most GTA 6 streamers will want once high-resolution console and PC captures become common; the budget pick trades passthrough resolution and interface speed for a lower price tier and a smaller footprint. We did not factor in prices or star ratings anywhere in this comparison — check current listings yourself and match the tier to your budget and how demanding your capture setup needs to be.

Best picture quality: Elgato 4K X

The 4K X is an internal PCIe capture card, which means your gameplay signal never leaves the machine to travel through an external passthrough box before hitting your capture software. That makes it the strongest pick for anyone building a dedicated capture or streaming PC and wanting full 4K60 HDR10 fidelity out of GTA 6 footage with as little added latency as possible.

  • Internal PCIe x4 capture card
  • 4K60 HDR10 passthrough and capture
  • Near-instant capture path since it sits inline with your GPU output
  • Broad compatibility with third-party capture and streaming software
  • Requires a free PCIe slot in a desktop tower

Best all-round: Elgato HD60 X

The HD60 X is an external USB card that covers the same 4K60 HDR10 passthrough ceiling as the premium pick, but without needing to open a case or dedicate a PCIe slot. That plug-and-play flexibility is why it's the best all-round choice for streamers who capture from a console, a laptop, or a second gaming PC running GTA 6.

  • External USB 3.0 capture card
  • 4K60 HDR10 passthrough with low added latency
  • Works across PC, PlayStation, and Xbox capture setups
  • Compatible with OBS, Elgato's own software, and most streaming suites
  • No internal installation required

Best budget: AVerMedia Live Gamer Mini

The Live Gamer Mini is a compact USB card built for straightforward 1080p60 capture rather than full 4K HDR passthrough. It's a sensible entry point if you mainly want to grab clips of GTA 6 moments for editing later rather than run a full HDR streaming setup, and it keeps the footprint on your desk minimal.

  • Compact external USB 2.0 capture card
  • 1080p60 passthrough (no 4K passthrough)
  • Plug-and-play with OBS and most capture software
  • Better suited to clip-focused capture than high-fidelity streaming
  • Sits at a budget-tier price point
