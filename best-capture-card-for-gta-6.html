Why a capture card matters for GTA 6

Rockstar has not detailed GTA 6's final console or PC output specifications. Based on the trailers released so far and the capabilities of the current console generation, it's reasonable to expect gameplay capable of 4K-class resolution and HDR color on PS5 and Xbox Series X — but treat any specific resolution or frame-rate figure as unconfirmed until Rockstar states it officially. A capture card with full HDMI passthrough keeps your own monitor or TV running at full resolution and refresh rate while a separate device handles recording, which matters for footage with the dense, neon-lit night scenes Vice Report has seen in the trailers so far. Weak passthrough or software-only capture tends to introduce lag or force you to downgrade your own display just to record.

FAQ

Do I need a capture card to record GTA 6 gameplay?

No — consoles and most gaming PCs include built-in recording and streaming tools. A capture card is worth it if you want higher bitrate, custom overlays, multi-track audio, or you're capturing from a device without native capture support.

Will GTA 6 run in 4K on PS5 and Xbox Series X?

Rockstar has not confirmed final resolution or frame-rate targets for GTA 6. Many current-generation titles ship with 4K-class modes, so a capture card with 4K60 HDR passthrough is a sensible choice, but treat any specific resolution claim for GTA 6 as unconfirmed until Rockstar states it officially.

Internal or external capture card for GTA 6 streaming?

External USB-C capture cards are simpler to set up with consoles and a separate streaming PC, while internal PCIe cards suit a single gaming PC that runs both the game and the capture software at once.
